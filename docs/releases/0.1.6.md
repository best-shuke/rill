## Fixes

- XPro nodes now dial absolute host names such as `example.com.` (as some clients send or the sniffer reports) instead of failing with "invalid XPro destination".
- A `#name` DNS outbound reports UDP support from the proxy group's live selection rather than the selection it had at startup.
- An XPro provider node that fails to parse is logged with its provider and node name, and an unnamed one is labelled `<unnamed>`, matching TrustTunnel.

## Downloads

- Linux: static musl binaries for x86_64 and ARM64.
- macOS: Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later.
- Windows: x86_64 and ARM64; Wintun is embedded.

`SHA256SUMS` lists the SHA-256 of every archive. The archives include rill's license, origin notice and third-party license notices.
